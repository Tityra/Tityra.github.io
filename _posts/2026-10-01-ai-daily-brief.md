## TOP 3

1. [Google announced Gemini 4 Argon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/) and is releasing it to trusted cyber defenders first rather than to the public.
2. [OpenAI and Synopsys signed a multi-year agreement](https://news.synopsys.com/2026-09-30-OpenAI-and-Synopsys-Announce-GPT-Synopsys-Frontier-Intelligence-to-Revolutionize-Chip-Design) to build GPT-Synopsys, a model specialised for chip design.
3. [The FTC has opened an investigation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/ftc-ai-probe-openai-anthropic.html) into OpenAI, Anthropic and METR over risks from agentic systems.

## Models

- **Gemini 4 Argon:** [Google's September 30 announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/) describes a frontier model for coding, enterprise knowledge work and cyber defence, with a maximum output of 1 million tokens.
  - Access is **phased**, starting with trusted cyber defenders through Google's Fairwind Program, and Google says it is engaged in the US government's voluntary process for pre-release model access. Broader availability goes to paid API customers and AI Ultra subscribers, with no date given.
  - Launch pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at 95% off. The 77.9% on DeepSWE v1.1 is Google's own figure.
- **GPT-Synopsys:** [Synopsys and OpenAI announced](https://news.synopsys.com/2026-09-30-OpenAI-and-Synopsys-Announce-GPT-Synopsys-Frontier-Intelligence-to-Revolutionize-Chip-Design) an expansive multi-year agreement to jointly develop a chip-design model, including a revenue-sharing arrangement and joint go-to-market.
  - OpenAI licenses Synopsys' EDA tools to train the model to operate them, read their output and iterate on designs.
  - Synopsys states customer data is not used to train the model, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is governed by configurable retention, audit and permission controls.
  - The release itself carries **no performance figures**. Speed and productivity numbers circulating in secondary coverage do not appear in the announcement.

## Agentic AI & Agent Skills

- **Regulators reach agent behaviour:** [CNBC reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/ftc-ai-probe-openai-anthropic.html) the FTC has opened an investigation into OpenAI, Anthropic and the evaluation group METR over risks from their AI products, and will issue civil investigative demands in the coming weeks.
  - This is reporting, not an FTC publication: the agency has not posted a statement, and the scope described comes from the outlet and its sources.
